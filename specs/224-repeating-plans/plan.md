# Plan 224: Repeating Plans

- **Status:** Draft
- **Spec:** [./spec.md](./spec.md)
- **Tasks:** [./tasks.md](./tasks.md)
- **Depends on:** 223

## What

Unlock **Repeat** on the Plan editor: Once / Weekly (on weekday of Due) / Monthly (on the Nth). Advance `dueOn` after accept-mode Save or Skip. **Save for next** copies tx info onto the Plan when Repeat is not Once. List chips show Weekly / Monthly only.

## Why

223 is one-shot. Rent and payday need a reminder that comes back without auto-posting (the 004 failure mode).

## Out of this slice

- Yearly, custom RRULE, end-date / occurrence cap
- Save for next posting a tx
- 225 / 226
